Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps developed by José Lamarão, including Soletra, WarrantyVault, and Plygo.

These apps are provided at no cost and intended for use as is. This page informs users about policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information for anyone using these apps.

Information Collection and Use

The apps themselves do not directly collect or store any personal data. However, third-party services integrated into the apps (such as advertising or analytics SDKs) may collect limited data automatically for functionality, analytics, or ad personalization.

These third-party services may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Anonymous usage data (e.g., screens viewed, time spent)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

Third-Party Services Used

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Ads)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Unity Ads (for mediation in Plygo)

Advertising and Analytics

The apps may display advertisements served by AdMob or Unity Ads. These networks may use anonymous identifiers to deliver relevant ads. You can disable ad personalization in your Google account or device settings.

Analytics (via Firebase) is used to understand basic usage patterns (e.g., number of sessions, feature usage) to improve app performance and user experience. No personally identifiable data is collected.

GDPR (European Union) Compliance

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) are informed that third-party partners may collect and process data (such as advertising identifiers) for personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage consent through the app’s settings or via your device’s privacy options.

CCPA (California, USA) Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users residing in California have the right to opt out of personalized advertising and the “sale” of personal data. You can control ad personalization through:

  • Google Ad Settings
  • Your device’s privacy or ad tracking settings

No personal data is ever sold or shared directly by these apps.

Data Retention and Security

No personal data is stored on servers controlled by the developer. Any data collected by third-party SDKs is governed by their own privacy policies. We rely on industry-standard security practices provided by these third parties.

Children’s Privacy

The apps are not directed toward children under 13 years of age. No data is knowingly collected from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. You are encouraged to review this page occasionally for any changes. Updates will be posted on this same URL.
