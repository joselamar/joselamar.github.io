Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps developed by José Lamarão, including Soletra, WarrantyVault, and Plygo.

These apps are provided at no cost and intended for use as is. This page informs users about policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information for anyone using these apps.

Information Collection and Use

The apps themselves do not directly collect or store any personal data. However, third-party services integrated into the apps (such as advertising or analytics SDKs) may collect limited data automatically for functionality, analytics, or ad personalization.

These third-party services may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • Anonymous usage data (e.g., screens viewed, time spent)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • IP address (for location-based consent requirements)

Third-Party Services Used

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Ads)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Unity Ads (for mediation in Plygo)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (for consent management)

Advertising and Analytics

The apps may display advertisements served by AdMob or Unity Ads. These networks may use anonymous identifiers to deliver relevant ads. You can manage ad personalization preferences through the app's Settings menu or your device settings.

Analytics (via Firebase) is used to understand basic usage patterns (e.g., number of sessions, feature usage) to improve app performance and user experience. No personally identifiable data is collected.

Managing Your Privacy Preferences

You have control over how your data is used for advertising and analytics. You can change your consent choices at any time by:

  1. Opening the app (Plygo, Soletra, or WarrantyVault)
  2. Tapping the Settings icon (top-left on home screen)
  3. Selecting "Privacy Options"
  4. Updating your preferences in the consent dialog

Your choices will be saved and applied immediately. You can change these settings at any time.

Additionally, you can manage ad personalization through:

  • Google Ad Settings
  • Your device's privacy or ad tracking settings
  • Resetting your advertising ID in device settings

GDPR (European Union) Compliance

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom are protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). When you first open the app, you will be presented with a consent dialog explaining what data is collected and how it's used.

Your rights under GDPR include:

  • Right to Access: Request information about data collected
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Object: Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Change your consent choices at any time

To exercise these rights, use the Privacy Options in the app's Settings menu or contact us at lamaraodeveloper@gmail.com.

US State Privacy Laws Compliance

Users in certain US states are protected under state privacy laws, including:

  • CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • VCDPA (Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act)
  • CPA (Colorado Privacy Act)
  • CTDPA (Connecticut Data Privacy Act)
  • UCPA (Utah Consumer Privacy Act)

Your Rights Under US State Privacy Laws:

  • Right to Know: What personal information is collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising: Disable personalized ads
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of privacy choices

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Under California law (CCPA), you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. While we do not directly sell your data, advertising partners may use your data for targeted advertising, which may be considered "sharing" under California law.

To opt out of personalized advertising (which stops the "sharing" of data with ad partners):

  1. Open the app and go to Settings
  2. Tap "Privacy Options"
  3. Adjust your consent preferences to disable personalized ads

You can also manage this through Google Ad Settings or your device's privacy settings.

Purpose of Collection:

Third Parties We Share With:

Data Retention and Security

No personal data is stored on servers controlled by the developer. Any data collected by third-party SDKs is governed by their own privacy policies and retention schedules. We rely on industry-standard security practices provided by these third parties.

Most data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.
