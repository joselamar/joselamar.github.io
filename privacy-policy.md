## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps developed by José Lamarão, specifically:

- **WarrantyVault** – an app to help organize product warranties  

Each app may use third-party tools necessary for advertising or purchases, as described below.

---

## 3. Information Collection and Use

I do **not collect or process any personal information**.  
The Apps do **not require registration**, **do not track you**, and **do not store any identifiable data** on my servers.

However, the Apps may use **third-party services** that operate independently and may collect limited, anonymous technical information to provide their features:

### Soletra
- Uses **Google AdMob** for displaying ads.  
  AdMob may collect device identifiers and analytics to deliver and measure ads.  
  This data is processed by Google, not by me.

### WarrantyVault
- Uses **RevenueCat** to manage in-app purchases.  
  RevenueCat processes purchase validation through Google Play or the App Store.  
  No personal data is shared with me — only anonymized purchase status.

#### Third-party privacy policies:
- [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)  
- [AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)  
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

## 4. Data Storage

All information entered by users (such as warranty details in WarrantyVault) is **stored locally on the device only**.  
No data is transmitted to me or any external server.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

- **WarrantyVault** is suitable for users aged **13 and above**.  

Neither app collects personal information from anyone, including children.
